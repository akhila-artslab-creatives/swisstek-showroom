Proposal · three directions

Three routes into the same showroom.

The proposal offers three ways to build the virtual showroom. All three are working below, so the choice can be made by using them rather than by reading about them. All three share one content layer: ten spaces, the same products, the same 25 grout shades, the same running specification. What changes is how the customer moves. The tour covers the finishing range, Roof Master, Swissparkett, decorative pebbles and Swisstek Aluminium.

A cinematic environment organised around the three Swisstek collections. The customer moves through ten spaces and each zone is a gateway to a product family, rather than every product being physically on display.

Direction 02
Working sample

Product-focused
360° showroom

A realistic showroom where products are selected directly from the scene. Point at the pack, open the pack. Product cards carry the real pack shot, pack size, standards and the full shade chart with family filtering, and products can be compared side by side without leaving the room. Swisstek's own films play inside the space: 39 usage guides, fabrication walkthroughs and product films pulled from the Swisstek Ceylon PLC and Swisstek Aluminium channels.

MovementTen spaces, click products in the room
StrengthDirect product discovery, video in the room, compare side by side
LimitationLayout changes need new renders

How to read these samples

Product photography, product names, pack sizes, standards and the 25 grout shade codes are real and come from Swisstek's own catalogue and website, including the Swisstek Aluminium range. The showroom environments are generated concept visualisation, standing in for the panoramas a production build would render properly, so packaging shown in the rooms is approximate. Every product card uses the real photograph. Direction 03 is genuinely live 3D, not a video.
